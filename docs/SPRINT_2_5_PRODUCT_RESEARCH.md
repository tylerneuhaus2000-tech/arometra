## Provenance and rights decision

- Retrieval date for this research pass: `2026-10-10`. For every future field, keep its own source, retrieval date, review status, and author/method; a page-level source does not prove every field.
- Manufacturer flavor sections are not a standardized 0–10 dataset. No numeric flavor value, community review, or recommendation signal was derived from this research.
- Pack sizes and ABV are market-specific variants. A brand/product candidate is not enough to assign a GTIN, bottle size, or one global alcohol percentage.
- The reviewed site terms reserve or limit reuse of site content. They do not establish a license for AROMETRA. See the site conditions for [Diageo](https://legal.diageo.com/en-us/website-conditions-of-use), [Brown-Forman](https://legal.brown-forman.com/terms-of-use/english), [Suntory Global Spirits](https://www.suntoryglobalspirits.com/terms-conditions), [BACARDÍ](https://www.bacardi.com/nl/nl/terms-and-conditions/), [Hendrick's](https://hendricksgin.com/terms-conditions/), [Absolut](https://www.absolut.com/en-us/legal/terms-of-use/), and [Proximo Spirits](https://proximospirits.com/fr/conditions-dutilisation/). These pages are rights warnings, not legal advice or a determination that factual fields are copyrightable.
- A possible open-data route is Open Food Facts, whose official documentation describes ODbL/database-content and separate image-license conditions. It was not queried or imported. AROMETRA must decide whether it can meet those obligations before use: [API documentation](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/), [license guidance](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/tutorials/license-be-on-the-legal-side/).
- Result: **0 approved sources, 0 real product rows in the AROMETRA catalog, 0 licensed product images**. No data was sent to Supabase.

## Next action before any real import

Select the initial market and exact variants, obtain a manufacturer/distributor export or written data permission (including separately scoped tasting-text and image rights), and have the license/attribution terms reviewed. Then store only supported fields in a candidate import, run the dry-run, and keep records private until per-field review.
